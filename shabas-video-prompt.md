# Google Flow prompt — Shabas marketing film (~40s)

> Paste the shots below into Google Flow one at a time. Flow generates roughly
> 8 seconds per clip, so this is six clips you assemble afterwards.
>
> **Generate the football and the neighbourhood. Do not ask it to generate the
> app.** AI-rendered phone screens look fake and the text comes out garbled.
> Shoot the phone from angles where the screen is dim, angled or out of focus,
> then drop the real app recordings in during the edit.

---

## House style — repeat this in every shot

```
Style: cinematic documentary, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of field, natural
handheld movement with slight weight. Golden-hour African sun, long shadows,
warm dust in the air. Colour grade: deep near-black greens in the shadows,
desaturated midtones, one vivid grass-green accent. Fine film grain. No
broadcast television look, no stadium production.
Setting: a neighbourhood football pitch in Douala, Cameroon — red-earth and
patchy grass, breeze-block walls, a hand-painted goal, palms and low rooftops
behind. Everyday clothes and mismatched kit, not matching professional strips.
Audio: ambient only — feet on dry earth, ball strikes, distant traffic, birds,
crowd murmur. No music, no voice-over.
```

## Shot 1 — Establishing (8s)

```
Wide establishing shot of a dusty neighbourhood football pitch at golden hour in
Douala. Twelve young men in mismatched kit warm up, long shadows stretching
across red earth. The camera pushes in slowly from behind a rusted goal, the
net swaying. Dust catches the low sun. Handheld, subtle drift.
[house style]
```

## Shot 2 — The challenge (8s)

```
Close shot of two team captains facing each other at the touchline, one holding
a phone at chest height, screen angled away from camera and out of focus. They
nod, shake hands, and both glance down at the phone. Shallow focus on their
faces, the phone soft in the foreground. Late afternoon light rims their
shoulders. Slow handheld push-in.
[house style]
```
*Leave the screen unreadable — the real app UI is composited here.*

## Shot 3 — The match (8s)

```
Low, fast tracking shot along the touchline as a winger drives the ball forward
on a dry earth pitch, defender closing in. Dust kicks up from studs. The camera
whips to follow the pass. Energetic handheld, motion blur, sun flaring across
the lens.
[house style]
```

## Shot 4 — The neighbourhood (8s)

```
Medium shot of spectators packed along the edge of the pitch — teenagers on a
low wall, women in bright print fabric, a man filming on his phone. They erupt,
arms up, as something happens off camera. Warm backlight through dust. Handheld,
slight rack focus from foreground faces to the pitch beyond.
[house style]
```

## Shot 5 — The result (8s)

```
Close shot after the final whistle: two sweating captains clasp hands, then one
holds up a phone between them and taps the screen once. Both look at it and
laugh. The referee walks past in the background. The screen stays dim and
angled away from the lens. Dusk light, cooler now. Static handheld.
[house style]
```
*The confirmation screen is composited here.*

## Shot 6 — End frame (8s)

```
Wide, slow shot of the empty pitch at dusk, one ball resting on the centre spot,
floodlight or streetlight just flickering on. Nobody in frame. Stillness. Very
slow push-in. Generous empty sky in the upper third of the frame.
[house style]
```
*Keep the sky clear — the logo and end card go there in the edit.*

## Negative prompt — paste into Flow's negative field

```
text, captions, subtitles, watermarks, logos, brand names, club crests,
readable phone screens, user interface, professional stadium, packed grandstand,
broadcast graphics, European league kit, drone hyperlapse, slow-motion cliché,
distorted hands, extra fingers, warped faces, plastic skin, oversaturated colour
```

## Settings

- **Aspect ratio:** generate the master in **16:9**, then regenerate Shots 1, 3
  and 5 in **9:16** for WhatsApp Status, Facebook Reels and TikTok. Reframing a
  16:9 clip to vertical crops the action badly.
- **Clips:** six at ~8s. Generate three or four variants of each and keep the
  best — Flow varies a lot between runs.
- **Resolution:** the highest Flow offers; you can always come down.

## In the edit — not in Flow

1. **Drop the real app recordings into Shots 2 and 5.** Screen-record the actual
   flow (send challenge → both captains accept → confirm the result) from the
   demo build, so the data is clean.
2. **Titles in Anton, body in Inter**, matching the site. Green `#2DB355` on
   near-black `#080B09`.
3. **Suggested captions**, matching the site's copy:
   - after Shot 1: *Votre quartier. Votre terrain.*
   - after Shot 3: *Vos résultats, vérifiés par les deux capitaines.*
   - on Shot 6: the Shabas logo, then `sha-bas.com` and the Play Store badge.
4. **Music:** something with Cameroonian percussion, not stock corporate. Keep
   the ambient audio underneath it.
5. **Cut a 15s version** (Shots 1, 3, 6) for ads — the 40s cut is for the site
   and your Facebook page.

## One caution

Flow will happily invent recognisable-looking club badges and kit. Check every
clip before publishing: anything resembling a real club's crest or a national
strip is a rights problem in an advert.
